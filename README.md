# Temporal Space in Institutional Reasoning

**An exploratory inquiry within Governance Engineering**

- Author: Sam Hayward, Governance Engineering
- Recorded: 9 October 2026
- Version: 0.1 — Exploratory Foundation
- Status: Exploratory; not validated; not a paper or implementation specification.
- No PHDSS code or contracts changed.

## Purpose

Investigate whether external instruments can extend human temporal perception: enabling people to recognise relationships across what happened, what might happen, what could have happened, and how others experience those trajectories.

## Two independent research streams

1. **Human Temporal Perception (primary):** Can an external instrument extend our capacity to perceive duration, change, branching, relationships and multiple temporal perspectives?
2. **Institutional Temporal Reasoning:** Can such capabilities improve how institutions understand and govern decisions and consequences?

The first stream remains independent of the second. Governance Engineering is one application; PHDSS is a possible future test environment, not the home of this inquiry.

## Canonical status

This repository is the canonical research home. Claude Projects, ChatGPT, Artifacts, Sites and other working environments are exploratory workspaces. Findings become part of the canonical record only through reviewed commits.

## Research discipline

- Distinguish observed events, interpretations and imagined futures.
- Analytical representation is not participation.
- A more complete representation is not necessarily a more adequate frame.
- Do not claim novelty or effectiveness without literature review and empirical evaluation.
- No PHDSS implementation changes are authorised by this research record.
